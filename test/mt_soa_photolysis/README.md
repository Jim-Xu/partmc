# Photolysis of the monoterpene SOA test case (MOSAIC)

This tests the photolysis of the monoterpene SOA in the particles by MOSAIC, a reaction of the CRACMM3 mechanism of CMAQ 6.0 (`SOAphot01`): the highly oxygenated molecules `AHOM` (C* 0.0063 ug/m3) turn into the more oxidized `AMTN1` (O:C 1.0, C* 0.1 ug/m3, vapor `VMTN1`) with a molar yield of 0.8 (a mass yield of 0.8 x 262 / 250), and 0.2 mol of formic acid per mole photolyzed goes to the gas phase, at 1% of the photolysis frequency of NO2.

The run is 1 h at 270 K and 30% relative humidity with a fixed photolysis frequency of NO2 of 0.008 s-1 (all other photolysis frequencies are zero), particles of ammonium sulfate and `AHOM`, and no reactive gases. Test 1 compares the mass concentrations of `AHOM` and `AMTN1` and the mixing ratio of formic acid with the analytic solution, `AHOM` = M0 exp(-0.01 J t); `compute_reference.py` writes the references in the column layouts of `extract_aero_time` and `extract_gas`, with zeros for all other species. A quarter of the `AHOM` is photolyzed. The tolerance of 5% covers the Monte Carlo sampling of the initial particles.
