
# Organic aerosol scenario

This scenario is the test bed for the organic aerosol scheme of MOSAIC: the precursors of every formation pathway of the scheme are present, and the processing program writes the organic aerosol diagnostics. It is independent of the other scenarios.

The case is a box: a well-mixed boundary layer of constant depth, defined by its environment, its initial air, the background air with which it exchanges, the emissions of the land beneath it and the dry deposition to that land (section The case below). The script `make_case.py` writes all input files, `organics.spec` included, from the parameters at its top. Other cases are set up by changing these parameters or by adding a variant to the script: `./make_case.py forest` writes the case without the city, `./make_case.py urban` the case without the biogenic emissions and `./make_case.py urban_forest_nodep` the case without dry deposition. Until November 2026 the scenario took the meteorology, the initial and background air, the emissions and the particle size distributions of `1_urban_plume` and added the emissions of the precursors of the organic aerosol.

The organic aerosol is that of the CRACMM3 mechanism of CMAQ 6.0 (Pye et al., 2023; Skipper et al., 2024): 24 semivolatile species (reactive organic carbon in volatility bins, alkane-like `AROCN2ALK` to `AROCP3ALK` and oxygenated `AROCN2OXY2` to `AROCP3OXY2`; the highly oxygenated molecules of the monoterpenes `AHOM` and `AELHOM`; the monoterpene nitrates `ATRPN` and `AHONIT`; the photolysis product of the HOM `AMTN1`; the aromatic peroxides `AOP3`) and 7 nonvolatile species (`AISO3NOS` and `AISO3OS` from the acid-catalyzed uptake of the isoprene epoxydiols, `AISO4` and `AISO5` from the uptake of the isoprene tetrafunctional compounds and nitrates, `AGLYOLIG` from the uptake of methylglyoxal, `ASOAT`, which the reactions of phenols, cresols and a few other gases form directly, and `AORGC` from cloud processing, which is not formed here; in the present case it stands for the aged regional organic matter of the initial and background particles). The 94 gas species of this organic aerosol block (the vapors of the semivolatile species, the precursors such as `SOATOL`, `SOAXYL`, `BENZ`, `NAPH`, `HC10`, `SESQ` and the IVOCs, and their radicals and products) react as tracers on the CBM-Z gas phase: their reactions read OH, HO2, NO, NO2, NO3, O3, CH3O2, C2O3 and `ISOPP` of CBM-Z but do not change them, and the reactions of the host species `API`, `LIM`, `BPIN`, `ISOP`, `ISOPP` and `ISPX` that form block species run at the rates of the host reactions. The species, the reactions and the properties of the aerosol species (C* at 298 K, enthalpy of vaporization, O:C and OM:OC; kappa = 0.11 OM:OC - 0.10 as in CMAQ, Pye et al., 2017) are generated from the mechanism file of CMAQ (`mech_cracmm3.def`) and its aerosol definitions (`ORG_DEFN.F`). In the particles, `ATRPN` and `AHONIT` hydrolyze to `AHOM` and nitric acid (lifetime 3 h) and `AHOM` photolyzes to 0.8 `AMTN1` and 0.2 formic acid at 1% of the photolysis frequency of NO2. The hydroxy hydroperoxides `ISHP` and the epoxydiols `IEPOX` follow CRACMM3 as species of the block: `ISHP` is formed at the rate of `ISOPP` + HO2 of CBM-Z and reacts with OH at the CRACMM3 rate constants to 0.44 `IEPOX` and 0.14 `IPX` (RAM07); `IEPOX` is also formed from the nitrates `ISON` (RAM10) and lost by OH (RAM17). The epoxydiols are taken up as in CMAQ by particles with an aqueous phase and form `AISO3OS` in the fraction that reacts with sulfate, taking one mole of sulfate out of the inorganic sulfate of the particle per mole, as in CMAQ (the rest forms `AISO3NOS`); the ammonium of that sulfate goes to the gas phase in the NH4/SO4 ratio of the particle, as the inorganic equilibrium of CMAQ releases it from neutralized particles; `IPX` and `INALD` are taken up at 2 and 0.5 times the rate of the epoxydiols, `INALD` releasing nitric acid. Since the block species are tracers, the nitrogen of the organic nitrates of the block is not taken from CBM-Z, and the nitric acid that the hydrolysis of `ATRPN` and `AHONIT` and the uptake of `INALD` release adds nitrogen to CBM-Z: in the default case 0.25 ppb in two days, about 1% of the nitrogen oxides of the urban increment (over the forest alone 0.06 ppb). The `ASOAT` that the gas-phase reactions form is counted in the gas species `PROD_ASOAT` and moved to the particles in every time step (CMAQ adds it to its accumulation mode), distributed over them as the condensation of a nonvolatile vapor of 200 g/mol.

In the gas phase, the reactions of the monoterpenes (`API`, `LIM`, `BPIN`) with OH, O3 and NO3 consume the oxidant and form the gas-phase products of the monoterpene reactions of CB6r5 (TERP and APIN in `cb6r5_ae7_aq`), written with the species of CBM-Z. In the MOSAIC library of 2012 these were tracer reactions: OH was not consumed and no radicals were formed, so that OH was too high wherever monoterpenes are an important sink of it. One gas species was added for this, `XO2N`, the organic peroxy radical that forms an organic nitrate with NO instead of NO2. A `gas_data.dat` without `XO2N` still works, but this radical is then lost at the end of every time step.

The isoprene peroxy radical (`ISOPP`) also follows CB6r5: its reaction with HO2 forms the hydroxy hydroperoxides `ISPX` with 0.12 OH and HO2 (in CBM-Z it formed the generic hydroperoxide `ROOH`), and it isomerizes to HO2 and the hydroperoxy aldehydes, the gas species `HPLD`, which photolyze to OH at 0.055 times the photolysis frequency of NO2. The reaction of `ISPX` with OH consumes OH and returns 0.933 OH; the epoxydiols that it forms in CB6r5 are not followed here (see the organic aerosol above). Under low NO these reactions return most of the radicals that the oxidation of isoprene consumed in CBM-Z. The three isoprene peroxy radicals (`ISOPP`, `ISOPN`, `ISOPO2`) react with NO at 2.7e-12 exp(350/T) cm3 s-1 and `ISOPP` reacts with HO2 at 2.12e-13 exp(1300/T) cm3 s-1, the measured rate constants (Wennberg et al., Chem. Rev. 118, 3337, 2018), instead of the CBM-Z values (4.0e-12 and 1.7e-13 exp(1300/T)); together with the isomerization these set how much of the isoprene ends as hydroxy hydroperoxides. A `gas_data.dat` needs `ISPX` and `HPLD`: without them the products of `ISOPP` + HO2 and of the isomerization are lost at the end of every time step.

Two photolysis frequencies also follow CB6r5 (ratios to that of NO2, from the cross sections and quantum yields of `cb6r5_ae7_aq`): methylglyoxal `MGLY` 1.41e-2 and the organic nitrates `ONIT` 3.2e-4. The CBM-Z values were 2.0 and 0.31 times these. In the urban plume that this scenario simulated before (the case of `1_urban_plume`, see above), the slower photolysis of `MGLY` lowered HO2 by about a tenth and the ozone maximum by a few percent; the organic aerosol did not change beyond the noise of the particle sampling, except for the SOA from methylglyoxal, which follows its gas concentration. The isoprene products `ISOPRD` (methacrolein, methyl vinyl ketone) photolyze at 3.67e-4 times the frequency of NO2: the cross sections of CMAQ (the mean of the two molecules) with the quantum yields recommended by the JPL evaluation 19-5 (Burkholder et al., 2020; measured by Gierczak et al., 1997), exp(-0.055 (lambda - 308 nm)) / (5.5 + 9.2e-19 [M]) for methyl vinyl ketone and 0.005 for methacrolein (recommended: below 0.01). CMAQ itself uses a quantum yield of 0.05 at all wavelengths for this species, an upper limit from an earlier study, which gives 2.87e-3 and would make this photolysis a tenth of the primary source of radicals where isoprene dominates; the CBM-Z value was 1.48e-4. The photolysis of `ISOPRD` also has the products of CB6r5 (R161), written with the species of CBM-Z: 1.10 HO2 + 0.50 XO2 + 0.34 CH3O2 + 0.208 C2O3 + 0.26 HCHO + 0.24 OLET + 0.24 PAR + 0.17 AONE + 0.128 ALD2, where ALD2 stands for the glycolaldehyde of CB6r5; CBM-Z formed 0.97 C2O3 and 0.7 CH3O2, that is more peroxyacetyl nitrate and less HO2.

Three further reactions of the radicals follow CMAQ. The peroxyacetyl radical reacts with HO2 as in CB6r5 (R57: 3.14e-12 exp(580/T) cm3 s-1, 0.5 OH + 0.5 CH3O2 + 0.13 O3 + 0.5 `RCOOH`); in CBM-Z this reaction ended the radical chain (0.4 O3 + 0.4 `RCOOH`). The reaction of the isoprene peroxy radical `ISOPP` with the other organic peroxy radicals forms 0.8 HO2, 0.598 HCHO and 0.072 `XO2` besides `ISOPRD` (CB6r5 R154); in CBM-Z it formed `ISOPRD` only. O(1D) is quenched by O2 and N2 at 3.3e-11 exp(55/T) and 2.15e-11 exp(110/T) cm3 s-1 (the values of the CRACMM3 mechanism of CMAQ 6.0, whose sum for air is the O1D + M of CB6r5) and reacts with water at 2.14e-10 cm3 s-1 (CB6r5 R11), so that an O(1D) atom forms 12% less OH than with the rate constants of CBM-Z. Together these lowered HO2 by 10 to 15% and the ozone maximum by 4% in that urban plume; where isoprene dominates, OH is 3% lower in the afternoon.

HO2 is lost on the particles as in the CRACMM3 mechanism of CMAQ 6.0 (reaction `HET_HO2`): without products, with the uptake coefficient 0.2 (Ivatt et al., 2022) and limited by gas-phase diffusion. PartMC computes the loss frequency of every time step from the wet diameter of each particle, k = sum of n pi D^2 / (D / (2 Dg) + 4 / (c gamma)), with the mean molecular speed c of HO2 and the gas-phase diffusivity Dg that CMAQ uses for its uptake reactions; CMAQ uses the wet surface area of its accumulation mode instead of the sum over all particles. The optional spec file line `ho2_uptake_coeff` (after `do_fixed_photolysis` and `iepox_acidity`) sets the uptake coefficient; the default is 0.2 and 0 switches the loss off. Uptake coefficients measured on ambient particles are 0.1 to 0.4, those on purely organic particles are ten times smaller (Taketani et al., 2012; Lakey et al., 2015). In that urban plume, with 50 ug m-3 of particles, the loss lowered HO2 by 4 to 20% during the day and by half at night, and the ozone maximum by 5% (122 instead of 129 ppb).

Things to be aware of:

* The depth of the box is constant: the boundary layer neither grows in the morning nor collapses at night, so the emissions of the night mix into 1000 m and the nighttime monoterpenes are lower than near the ground.
* Apart from the biogenic gases nothing is emitted after the start. The urban increment decreases by chemistry, coagulation, deposition and the exchange with the regional air (by the exchange alone to 1/e^2 after two days).
* Only the gases with measured deposition velocities and the oxygenated vapors are deposited. O3, NO2, SO2, NH3, HCHO, PAN, the aldehydes and the alkane-like vapors, which CMAQ also deposits, are not. Without deposition (`urban_forest_nodep`) the long-lived products accumulate over the forest: the epoxydiols `IEPOX` reach 0.85 ppb on the second afternoon, and with them the isoprene SOA.
* The particle deposition assumes neutral stability and a constant wind speed, so that the particles deposit at the same velocity day and night; the nighttime gas deposition velocities (a tenth of the daytime values) are an assumption.
* The biogenic gases start from zero at 06:00, so the first morning has fewer monoterpenes than a morning after a night of emissions.
* The urban POA is in the particles at the start, as far as the POA mass goes, and then evaporates according to the gas-particle partitioning; CMAQ instead emits the more volatile part of each profile directly as vapor. The total is the same.
* The black carbon of the urban particles is 7.4 ug m-3 per ppm of CO, from the compositions of the diesel and gasoline particles of `1_urban_plume`.
* The regional air, the temperature, the exchange rate and the land cover are idealized. The emission factors of Table 2 of MEGAN2.1 are global averages of each plant functional type; MEGAN2.1 itself uses emission factors that vary by ecoregion.
* The sky is clear: the photolysis and the biogenic emissions are those of a cloudless day.

## Running

    ./make_case.py        # only to change the case: writes the input files, see above
    ./1_run.sh
    ./2_process.sh
    ./3_plot.sh

PartMC must be built with the MOSAIC library that has the CRACMM3 organic aerosol. The default run (3 repeats of 1000 particles, 48 h) takes about a quarter of an hour; results that are to be interpreted need at least 1000 particles and several repeats.

`organics_process.F90` writes `out/organics_process.nc` with the time series (averaged over the repeats) of

* `mass_conc_<group>`: mass concentration of the species groups of CMAQ's species definitions for CRACMM3 (`SpecDef_Conc_cracmm3.txt`): `poa` (the alkane-like reactive organic carbon, APOM of CMAQ, and `OC`), `soa_roc` (the oxygenated reactive organic carbon, `AOP3` and `ASOAT`, AORGA of CMAQ: SOA from aromatics, alkanes, IVOCs and sesquiterpenes, but also the oxygenated POA of cooking, which is emitted as these species), `soa_monoterpene`, `soa_isoprene` (together AORGB of CMAQ), `soa_glyoxal` and `soa_cloud`,
* `tot_org_mass_conc`, `tot_sec_org_mass_conc`: all organic matter and secondary organic matter (everything except the group `poa`, as ASOM of CMAQ),
* `tot_o_c`, `tot_om_oc`: carbon-weighted O:C and OM:OC of all organic matter; `sec_o_c`: O:C of the secondary organic matter,

and one file per output time with the two-dimensional number distributions of dry diameter against the per-particle O:C (`diam_o_c_dist`) and against the secondary organic fraction of the dry mass (`diam_sec_frac_dist`).
